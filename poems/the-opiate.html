Sonnet: The Opiate

by Krishna Patel

Shall I compare thee to the poppy's bloom?
Thou art more constant, sweet, and merciful:
Cold winds may shake the censer's ashen plume,
And prayer itself grow faint and dutiful.
At times too fierce the chemist's furnace glows,
And oft the saint's bright vision waxeth dim;
Each creed must fall, each perfect reason knows
No truth survives unspoiled by doubt or whim.
But thy soft benediction shall not fade,
Nor lose the hush that mortals dearly crave;
Nor shall the needle boast it lent thee shade,
When kneeling hearts find peace within thy grave.
So long as want endures and children weep,
So long lives this—the dream they take for sleep.

Krishna Patel
