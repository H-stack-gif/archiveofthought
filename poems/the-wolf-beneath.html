The Wolf Beneath the Floorboards

by Krishna Patel

What grows more fierce for being locked away?
The wolf beneath the floorboards knows its name,
And paces through the silence night and day
While you above speak only words of shame.

How much of strength is spent to hold the door,
To nail it shut, to stack the furniture high—
That ravenous and faithful beast you swore
You'd starve to death before you'd catch its eye.

But hunger makes a mirror. On your knees
You'll find the boards are thin as moth-wing skin,
And what you feared was fang and disease
Is fur and beating heart—the twin within.

Let up the latch. Unbar the splintered frame.
You'll learn what good men do with claws and flame.

Krishna Patel
